About

Jonathan Roth

Commercial real estate investor, hospitality operator, and founder of Harrington Oak.

Jonathan brings 15+ years of hands-on experience across real estate, hospitality, food service, and business acquisitions. His background spans both the United States and Europe, working on property evaluations, operator partnerships, and complex deal structures. He understands the full lifecycle of commercial real estate — from identifying opportunity and evaluating fundamentals to executing transactions and managing operations. This experience has shaped his practical, owner-operator perspective when reviewing properties and working with investors and business owners.

Through Harrington Oak, Jonathan focuses on identifying and structuring opportunities across the Southeast, particularly in retail, hospitality, mixed-use, and redevelopment. He believes the best deals happen through direct relationships, honest evaluation, and a clear understanding of what matters to both sides. Whether you're considering a sale, exploring a partnership, or simply trying to understand what your property is worth, Jonathan approaches every conversation with a straightforward mindset: understand the property, understand your goals, and determine the best path forward.
